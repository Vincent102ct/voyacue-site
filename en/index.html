Highlights

One companion for better photos and memorable trips

VoyaCue brings photography inspiration together with simple, practical travel-planning tools.

Explore poses

Browse 440 templates and find a scene and reference image that fit your photo session.

Shoot with a reference

Guided Camera shows the selected reference photo and, for supported poses and modes, a Guide Pose line drawing. Use these visual references to arrange the shot yourself.

Plan your trip

Choose places, organize them by day and save an itinerary for your next journey.

Personal photo templates

Keep the ideas you love

Import images from your iPhone to make your own pose references. Organize them with names, tags and collections, and reuse them whenever you want.

Destinations & food

More ideas for where to go

Find places, view routes when connected to the internet and explore local food suggestions by destination.
