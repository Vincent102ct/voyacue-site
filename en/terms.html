VoyaCue Terms of Use

Important information about app usage, Free and Pro plans, and purchases through the Apple App Store.

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Developer: Lang Khoa Tran

App license: VoyaCue uses Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (Standard EULA) ↗. The information below explains VoyaCue’s features and subscription transactions; it does not replace any mandatory Apple terms.

1. What does VoyaCue offer?

VoyaCue helps you explore photo poses, take reference-guided photos in supported modes, manage personal reference photos, create travel itineraries, and discover places and food. Some functions require Camera, Photos, Location permission or an internet connection.

2. VoyaCue Free and VoyaCue Pro

PlanAccess
Free109 pose templates and up to 5 personal photo templates, plus other free features available in the app
ProAll 440 pose templates and unlimited personal photo templates while a valid subscription is active

Automated pose scoring is not available for every template. Supported functions vary by template and mode.

3. Auto-renewable subscriptions

VoyaCue Pro offers weekly, monthly and yearly subscriptions. The applicable plan name, duration, full price and currency are shown inside the app before you confirm a purchase through Apple In-App Purchase.

  • Subscriptions renew automatically each billing period unless you cancel renewal under Apple’s terms.
  • You can view, change or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • Canceling renewal does not automatically request a refund. Pro access will generally remain available until the end of the paid period, provided Apple still reports an active entitlement.
  • To request a refund, visit Apple’s refund portal ↗.
  • If a purchase has not unlocked Pro, use Restore Purchases in the app.

4. Your content and data

You are responsible for using reference photos and other content you have the right to use. When importing images or information related to others, respect applicable privacy and intellectual property rights.

Deleting a template in VoyaCue is not designed to delete its original photo in your iPhone Photos library. See our Privacy Policy for information about your data and choices.

5. Third-party information

Place listings, routes, travel times and other travel information may be supplied by third-party services and may change over time. Verify important information with official sources before traveling.

6. Support
