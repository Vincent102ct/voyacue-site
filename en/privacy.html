Privacy Policy

How VoyaCue accesses device features, stores information and works with third-party services.

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · App: VoyaCue iOS · Developer: Lang Khoa Tran

At a glance: VoyaCue does not require an account. Reference photos and pose data are analyzed on your iPhone; personal templates and trip plans are primarily kept in the app’s private storage. When you use maps, payments or certain SDKs, technical information may be processed by their providers as explained below.

1. Data VoyaCue processes

VoyaCue 1.0 does not offer VoyaCue accounts or VoyaCue cloud synchronization. We do not operate a separate server that receives users’ original photos for pose analysis. MediaPipe models process image inputs on device.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera: used to take photos and offer pose guidance when you open the relevant feature.
  • Photos: lets you select reference photos; write access is used when a corresponding save-photo feature is invoked.
  • Location: used when you request nearby places or a feature that needs your location.

You can revoke these permissions in iPhone Settings → VoyaCue. Denying a permission may prevent the feature that requires it from working, but other features may remain available.

3. Third-party services

Google MediaPipe (pose analysis)

VoyaCue integrates MediaPipe Tasks Vision to analyze images on device. According to Google’s MediaPipe Tasks privacy notice ↗, image and video inputs are not sent to Google to perform the analysis. Google also states that MediaPipe Tasks APIs send performance, usage and technical metrics for maintenance, improvement and troubleshooting. This may include characteristics of the app and system, SDK usage frequency and performance measurements. Processing of these metrics is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy ↗. The actual scope of the transmitted data may depend on the SDK version and operating environment. We assess applicable notice and consent requirements.

Apple Maps / MapKit and map links

When you search for a place, request nearby results or calculate a route, Apple Maps may process the necessary query and location information to return results. If you open an external mapping app such as Google Maps, that service’s separate privacy policies apply.

Apple Privacy ↗ · Google Privacy ↗

Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases)

Apple processes subscription payments. VoyaCue uses transaction and entitlement information supplied by StoreKit to enable the appropriate Free or Pro features. We do not ask you to provide payment card numbers directly to VoyaCue.

Website and hosting

This marketing and support website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process website connection data according to Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy ↗.

4. When you contact support

If you email us voluntarily, we receive your email address, request details and any information or attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to reply, investigate issues and process legitimate support requests. Email providers may process information under their own policies. Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment card details or unnecessary private photos.

5. Storage and deletion

Personal templates, photos and trip plans remain in the app’s data storage until you delete them or remove the app and its data. Deleting a template in VoyaCue does not delete the original photo from your iPhone Photos library. Backups created through Apple or by the user may exist separately and are managed under Apple’s policies. Information processed by providers such as Apple and Google is subject to their retention rules. Support emails are kept as long as needed to resolve requests and related obligations; you may request deletion when appropriate.

6. Your choices

  • Revoke Camera, Photos or Location permissions in iPhone Settings.
  • Manage or delete reference templates and itineraries using the app’s features.
  • Manage your Pro subscription in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • Contact us to request access to or deletion of information you sent through support email.

7. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when VoyaCue’s features or data practices change. The update date appears at the top of this page. Where required by law, additional notices or consent requests will be provided through appropriate channels.
