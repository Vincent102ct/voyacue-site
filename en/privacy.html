Transparency & data protection

Privacy Policy

How VoyaCue accesses device features, stores information and works with third-party services.

Version 1.0 · Last updated 7 November 2026

VoyaCue helps you use photo references, follow pose guides, take photos and plan trips. You do not need an account. VoyaCue is published by the developer named on its App Store page. This policy covers VoyaCue for iOS, version 1.0. Questions and requests: voyacue.support@gmail.com

Summary

  • Your photos, pose processing results and saved trips stay on your device. VoyaCue has no server that receives them.
  • Photos you take are saved to your iOS Photos library automatically.
  • Location is used only while you use the app, for nearby travel and food features.
  • Map searches are sent to Apple. Google Maps actions open Google Maps or your browser.
  • VoyaCue Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment.
  • VoyaCue 1.0 has no accounts, advertising or analytics SDK.

Photos and camera

You choose individual reference images with the iOS photo picker. VoyaCue receives only the images you select; it cannot browse your photo library. Camera access is used to show the viewfinder and take photos. VoyaCue does not record audio or video.

To guide your pose, VoyaCue processes camera frames and selected images on your device with bundled MediaPipe and Core ML models. This processing estimates body landmarks and creates subject masks and line guides so the app can guide you toward the reference pose you chose. Body landmarks are used only for pose guidance, not to recognize who you are. VoyaCue does not send your images or landmarks to ChatGPT, Google, a VoyaCue server or any other service, and does not use them to train models. The bundled pose illustrations were created with ChatGPT, as declared by the app owner; ChatGPT is not used to analyze your photos.

When you tap the shutter, VoyaCue automatically saves the original JPEG to your iOS Photos library. iOS may ask for permission to add photos. If saving fails or permission is denied, VoyaCue keeps a local copy so you can retry. Retake removes VoyaCue's own copy and returns to the camera; it does not remove a photo already saved to Photos. Use the Photos app to view, share or delete saved photos. The JPEG may include camera metadata; VoyaCue does not add your GPS location to photos.

Data stored on your device

VoyaCue stores saved reference images, thumbnails, pose guides, body landmarks, template details, tags, collections, favorites, saved concepts and trip plans in its app storage and a local database. A trip plan can contain place names, addresses, coordinates, dates, transport mode and notes. VoyaCue also keeps local safety copies of captured photos and records of which photos were already saved to Photos, so the same photo is not saved twice. This data has no automatic expiry and is not uploaded to VoyaCue. Temporary files are cleaned up during normal use; if an operation is interrupted, a temporary file can remain until a later cleanup.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual references, saved concepts and trips with their delete controls in the app.
  • To remove all VoyaCue data from this device, including capture safety copies, delete the app. "Offload App" in iOS Settings keeps the app's data.
  • Photos already saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them in the Photos app.
  • Device backups and iCloud Photos follow your Apple settings; VoyaCue cannot delete copies held there.

VoyaCue does not keep a copy of this data anywhere else, so we cannot delete or export it for you.

Location

With your permission, VoyaCue reads your location while you use the app to find nearby travel highlights, run Explore searches and choose the area for food suggestions. The location is precise unless you turn off Precise Location for VoyaCue in iOS. VoyaCue does not use location in the background and does not keep a location history. Explore can keep receiving location updates while the app is open. You can deny or change Location access in iOS Settings; browsing the catalog, the camera and manual trip planning work without location.

Maps and external services

Matching nearby places against VoyaCue's built-in catalog happens on your device. Online place search, address lookup and route estimates use Apple's map services (MapKit and Core Location). Depending on the action, Apple receives your search text, a search area or coordinates, the language, and a route's start, end and transport mode. VoyaCue keeps search results in memory for reuse for up to ten minutes; they are not written to storage and are cleared when the app is fully closed. Apple handles these requests under its own policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/

When you tap a Google Maps action, VoyaCue opens Google Maps or your browser with a link that contains your search text or place details. Nearby searches and the current-location map also include your current coordinates. Google and your browser handle that request under their own policies, and no results come back to VoyaCue. Links to data sources and licenses also open in your browser. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

VoyaCue Pro subscriptions

VoyaCue Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and handles your Apple ID and payment details; VoyaCue does not receive them.

To show and apply your plan, VoyaCue reads the subscription status that Apple's StoreKit verifies on your device: the plan you bought, when the current period started and ends, whether and when it renews, a plan change you scheduled, and whether the purchase was made in Apple's test environment (Sandbox). VoyaCue keeps this status in memory while the app runs and reads it again from StoreKit at the next launch. It does not save the status to its own storage and does not send it to a VoyaCue server or any other service. Restore Purchases asks the App Store for your current status again.

You can change or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings. Your photos, references and trips stay on your device when Pro ends. Apple handles purchases under its own policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Pose processing library (MediaPipe)

VoyaCue includes Google's MediaPipe library (version 1.2.2) to run pose models on your device. VoyaCue does not send your camera frames or images to Google. MediaPipe's terms state that its APIs send metrics about API performance and usage to Google, which processes them under the Google Privacy Policy. The terms describe this usage data as SDK engagement (such as installs and session counts), usage and performance (such as inference counts and hardware performance), application and input metadata (such as the app ID and general characteristics of processed media) and the system environment (host system and version): https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/legal/tos

We have not confirmed whether the MediaPipe version bundled in VoyaCue sends such metrics. We will update this policy if we learn that it does.

Advertising and analytics

VoyaCue 1.0 does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and has no accounts. Its only in-app purchase is the VoyaCue Pro subscription described above. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Contact and requests

Email voyacue.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message through Google's Gmail service. We use them only to reply to you and keep them only as long as needed to handle your request. Please do not send photos or personal information we do not need.

Changes to this policy

We will publish changes on the VoyaCue privacy policy page and in the app, and update the date at the top. The app includes a copy of this policy that you can read without a network connection.
