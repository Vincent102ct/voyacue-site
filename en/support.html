Questions? We’re here to help.

Find answers to common questions or contact VoyaCue support by email.

Contact VoyaCue

If you encounter an issue or need help with features, subscriptions or privacy, email us at:

voyacue.support@gmail.com ↗

VoyaCue does not require an account. You do not need to provide any password to get support.

1. Camera or Guide Pose is not showing

  1. Go to iPhone Settings → VoyaCue and make sure Camera access is enabled.
  2. Close the camera screen and reopen it in the app.
  3. Try in good lighting, keeping the person fully visible in the frame.
  4. If the Guide Pose is not shown for the selected template, try another template.

Guided Camera shows a reference photo and a Guide Pose line drawing for supported templates and modes. Use them as visual references to arrange the shot yourself.

2. Cannot import or save a reference photo

  1. Select the photo again using the iPhone photo picker.
  2. Make sure your device has sufficient available storage.
  3. If you use Free, check the five personal templates limit.
  4. If you have Pro but your access is not updated, try Restore Purchases.

Deleting a template in VoyaCue is not intended to delete the original photo from your iPhone Photos library.

3. I paid, but Pro has not been unlocked

  1. Check your internet connection.
  2. Open the VoyaCue Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Check your VoyaCue subscription in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  4. If the issue continues, email us with your plan name, VoyaCue version, approximate time of the issue, and an optional redacted screenshot of the message.
Cancellation and refunds: Manage or cancel automatic renewal in your Apple Account subscriptions. To request a refund, use Apple’s refund request portal ↗. See our Terms of Use.

4. Cannot find a place or route

  • Check your internet connection.
  • If using nearby features, check that you have granted Location permission.
  • Try a different spelling of the city or place name.
  • Routes, opening hours and map results can vary by region and service provider.

5. Manage or delete personal data

Reference photos, templates and trips are primarily stored on your device. You can delete individual items using the relevant app features. Uninstalling the app typically removes data stored in its private app container; iOS backups may be managed separately by Apple.

If you previously emailed support and wish to request access to or deletion of that information, contact voyacue.support@gmail.com. Read our Privacy Policy.

6. What to include in a bug report

  • Your VoyaCue app version and iOS version.
  • Your iPhone model.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem and the expected result.
  • Any error message or screenshot, with personal information hidden where appropriate.

Never send passwords, one-time passwords (OTP), card numbers or unnecessary private photos.
